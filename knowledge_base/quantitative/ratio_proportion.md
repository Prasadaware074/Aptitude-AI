# Quantitative Aptitude: Ratio and Proportion

## Overview
A Ratio is a comparison of two quantities by division. A Proportion is an equality of two ratios.

## Formulas
1. `Ratio a:b = a / b`.
2. **Compound Ratio**: The compound ratio of `a:b` and `c:d` is `(a*c) : (b*d)`.
3. **Proportion**: If `a:b = c:d`, then `a * d = b * c` (Product of extremes = Product of means).
4. **Mean Proportional**: Mean proportional between `a` and `b` is `sqrt(a * b)`.
5. **Third Proportional**: Third proportional to `a` and `b` is `c = b^2 / a`.

## Shortcuts
- Dividing a total quantity `T` into ratio `a:b:c`:
  - Share of A = `[a / (a + b + c)] * T`.
