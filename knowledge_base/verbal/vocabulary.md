# Verbal Ability: Vocabulary & Word Power

## Overview
Verbal ability evaluates word meanings, synonyms, antonyms, root words, contextual usage, and sentence completion.

## Key Concepts
1. **Synonyms**: Words with identical or nearly identical meanings (e.g., Meticulous = Precise).
2. **Antonyms**: Words opposite in meaning (e.g., Candid vs Deceitful).
3. **Etymology & Root Words**:
   - `Bene` = Good (Benevolent, Benefactor)
   - `Mal` = Bad (Malevolent, Malicious)
   - `Chron` = Time (Chronological, Anachronism)

## Essential Exam Words
- **Meticulous**: Showing great attention to detail; very careful and precise.
- **Ubiquitous**: Present, appearing, or found everywhere.
- **Pragmatic**: Dealing with things sensibly and realistically based on practical considerations.
- **Ephemeral**: Lasting for a very short time.
